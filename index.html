01 About Callahan Detail

The Same Crew.
Every Single Detail.

Most detail shops are a revolving door. Ours isn't. Kevin hired his four core detailers when they were young and just starting out, and made it his mission to train them into the best detailers, and the best professionals, in the industry.

He taught them everything: how to read a finish under the lights, how to correct paint without cutting corners, and how to treat every car, boat and bike as if it were their own. Years later, those same four still lead the crew. They've grown into seasoned pros, and today they're the ones training every new member of the team, passing down the skills and standards Kevin set from the very beginning.

So whether you drop off at our shop on Mattakeesett Street or we meet you at the marina, the people working on your vehicle have spent years learning to do it right, together.

      08 FAQ

      Questions,
      Answered

      Don't see yours? Call 781-635-2928 and we'll walk you through it.

      Do I need to bring my vehicle to your shop, or do you come to me?

      For auto detailing, motorcycle detailing, paint correction, ceramic coating and XPEL installations, vehicles come to our Pembroke shop at 43 Mattakeesett St so we can work in a controlled space with the right lighting and equipment. Marine detailing is the exception: we travel to your boat’s location for hull cleaning, shrink wrapping and other marine services.

      How long does a full auto detail take?

      A Complete Detail Package typically takes a few hours depending on your vehicle’s size and condition. The Ultimate Detail Package, with full paint correction and ceramic sealant, takes longer. We’ll give you a time estimate when you drop off your vehicle.

      What affects the price of my detail?

      Pricing depends on the size of your vehicle, its current condition, and which package or add-ons you choose. Heavily soiled interiors, pet hair or oxidised paint may need extra time and products, which we always discuss with you before we start.

      How long does ceramic coating installation take?

      A Tier 1 exterior-only coating typically takes a full day, while Tier 2 and Tier 3 packages with paint correction, interior coating and wheel removal can take two to three days.

      Can I combine ceramic coating with paint protection film?

      Yes. Many customers pair XPEL paint protection film on high-impact areas with a ceramic coating over the rest of the vehicle for maximum protection and gloss. We’ll walk you through which combination makes sense for how you drive.

      Will paint correction remove deep scratches?

      Paint correction removes fine scratches, swirl marks and light imperfections in the clear coat. Deep scratches that go through the clear coat into the base colour may reduce but not fully disappear; in some cases touch-up paint or a repaint is the better solution.

      How soon can I roll down my windows after tinting?

      Wait at least three to five days before rolling down freshly tinted windows so the adhesive can fully cure. Rolling them down too soon can cause the edges to lift.

      How is pricing determined for boat detailing?

      Pricing is calculated per linear foot of the vessel and adjusted based on current condition. A boat with heavy oxidation or years without correction costs more than one on a regular maintenance schedule, since it needs additional compounding.

      When should I schedule boat shrink wrapping?

      Most South Shore boaters book shrink wrapping in October and early November, before the first hard freeze. Boats under 30 feet are $25 per foot; boats over 30 feet or with a tower or flybridge are $30 per foot.

      How long does marine ceramic coating or PPF last?

      Marine ceramic coating typically holds hydrophobic protection and gloss for one to two boating seasons depending on usage and storage. XPEL marine-grade paint protection film lasts significantly longer and can be replaced in sections if an area takes damage.
